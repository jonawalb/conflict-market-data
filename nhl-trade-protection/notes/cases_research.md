### 2. Connor Hellebuyck (WPG): TRADED to New Jersey on Fri **Oct 23, 2026**. Status: VERIFIED
- **Request:** made privately in the offseason. He announced it publicly on **Aug 27, 2026** in a statement posted to his agent's X account. He skipped camp and was **suspended Sept 16** (first NHL player suspended for not reporting to camp in 27 years, per reports).
  - Cheveldayoff: "A contract is a contract" (headline); "No door is closed."

  - NMC: Hellebuyck said NJ was an acceptable destination under his no-trade protection (NHL.com). The exact clause wording (NMC vs NTC) was not confirmed in the pages I fetched, so that detail is PARTIAL.
- **Return to WPG:** Dawson Mercer, Nick Bjugstad, G Nico Daws, rights to Alexander Command, 2029 2nd, 2027 7th. https://www.nhl.com/news/trade-to-new-jersey-devils-going-to-fit-perfectly-for-connor-hellebuyck ; https://puckpedia.com/trade/1212 (title only; the page returned 403) ; https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/50260103/nhl-hellebuyck-trade-grades-devils-jets-mercer-bjugstad-command-daws
